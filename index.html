What conditions does Devshri Metabolics treat?

The clinic provides care for diabetes, lifestyle disorders, metabolic conditions and general health concerns. Contact the clinic to confirm availability for your specific needs.

When should I consult a diabetologist?

You may consider a consultation for elevated blood sugar, diabetes symptoms or difficulties managing existing diabetes. Seek prompt medical attention for urgent or severe symptoms.

Are personalized diabetes plans available?

Yes. According to the clinic website, treatment plans are tailored to each patient's condition, lifestyle and needs.

Does the clinic provide laboratory tests?

Yes. The website lists a range of diagnostic tests, including diabetes and general health testing. Contact the clinic for exact test availability.

Can I arrange a video consultation?

The clinic lists virtual consultations as a service. Please call the clinic to verify current availability, pricing and the booking process.

How can I book an appointment?

Use the Book Appointment link to visit the clinic's existing Eka Care profile, or call +91 95939 40301. Please confirm the location and available time slots with the clinic.
