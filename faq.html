Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before your visit.

What conditions does Devshri Metabolics treat?

The clinic provides care for diabetes, metabolic health concerns and general lifestyle-related conditions. Patients should confirm specific needs with the clinic before visiting.

When should I consult a diabetologist?

You may benefit from a consultation if you have elevated blood sugar, symptoms of diabetes, or difficulty managing an existing condition. Seek urgent medical attention for severe symptoms.

Are personalized plans available?

Yes. Care planning is shaped around the patient’s condition, history, lifestyle and goals, enabling more targeted and practical support.

Does the clinic provide laboratory tests?

Yes. According to the clinic’s services, laboratory and diagnostic support is available for diabetes and general health testing. Please confirm exact tests and timings with the clinic.

Can I arrange a video consultation?

Yes, the clinic lists video consultation support among its services. Availability may vary, so it is best to verify current availability and booking procedures by phone or through the appointment link.

How do I book an appointment?

Appointments can be arranged through the clinic’s external booking link or by calling the clinic at +91 95939 40301. Please verify timing and availability before visiting.
